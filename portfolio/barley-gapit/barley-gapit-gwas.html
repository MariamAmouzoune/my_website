This project interprets the published study “Genome-Wide Association Study of Leaf Rust Resistance at Seedling and Adult Plant Stages in a Global Barley Panel” (Amouzoune et al., Agriculture, 2022, 12, 1829). The AM2017 diversity panel included 316 released cultivars, advanced breeding lines, and landraces. The study combined leaf-rust phenotypes with 36,793 quality-controlled SNP markers to identify genomic regions associated with resistance to Puccinia hordei.

This page distinguishes the paper's reported results from any new rerun. A reproducible reanalysis requires the original genotype, phenotype, marker-map, and covariate files, along with explicit sample-ID matching.

01

Phenotypes and study design

Seedling response and adult-plant resistance

  • SRT-MRC: seedling response to the SRT-MRC isolate.
  • SRT-SAT: seedling response to the SRT-SAT isolate.
  • APR-SAT17: adult-plant resistance assessed at Sidi Allal Tazi in the 2017 cropping season.
  • APR-SAT19: adult-plant resistance assessed at Sidi Allal Tazi in the 2019 cropping season.

The seedling and adult-plant phenotypes represent different stages and assessment contexts. Associations should therefore be interpreted trait by trait; a signal for one isolate or stage is not automatically evidence of broad-spectrum resistance.

02

Models reported in the paper

In GAPIT3, the study compared six approaches: GLM, MLM, SUPER, MLMM, FarmCPU, and BLINK. In TASSEL 5.2.53, the tested models included GLM(Q), GLM(PCA), MLM(Q+K), and MLM(K+PCA). The final TASSEL association analysis used MLM(K+PCA), which controlled false positives more effectively than the tested GLM alternatives.

These tools and model names should not be conflated: the article reports GAPIT3 model comparisons and a final TASSEL MLM(K+PCA) analysis. A new GAPIT3 run is a complementary analysis unless its results have been verified against the published outputs.

Loci described as novel

The study identified genomic regions associated with resistance at both seedling and adult-plant stages, including regions on chromosome 2H (106.53 cM and 107.37 cM) and chromosome 7H (126.7 cM). Of the 58 reported MTAs, 26 had been reported in previous studies and 32 were described as novel in the paper. These are published findings, not a claim that this portfolio page independently reran and recovered all associations.

04

How to interpret GWAS results

  • Manhattan plot: peaks indicate markers with stronger statistical evidence of association; significance must be judged against the stated threshold and multiple-testing procedure.
  • Q–Q plot: compares observed and expected p-values and helps assess model calibration and potential inflation.
  • Effect and R²: indicate direction and estimated contribution under the fitted model; confirm the phenotype coding before describing an allele as resistance-increasing or susceptibility-increasing.
  • Candidate genes: nearby annotations provide hypotheses for follow-up, not proof of causality.
  • Breeding relevance: markers and resistant accessions may support marker-assisted selection after independent validation across genetic backgrounds and environments.
⚠️
Before claiming exact reproduction

Align sample IDs across phenotype, genotype, marker map, and structure covariates; document SNP QC and the exact model; verify the installed GAPIT3 version; and validate each Manhattan/QQ plot and reported MTA table against the paper. Do not call a marker significant merely because it crosses a line copied from a figure.
